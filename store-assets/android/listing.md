## App name

## Short description

## Full description

Enter ChessPerfect, a complete chess ecosystem for players, students, coaches and academies.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Challenge players in multiplayer chess.
- Train against a computer opponent with classical, rapid, blitz and bullet time controls.
- Join tournaments in supported competitive formats.

LEARN AND IMPROVE
- Solve tactical puzzles selected for your level.
- Explore positions on a full analysis board with engine lines and variations.
- Create positions with the board editor.
- Build and review your personal chess database.

YOUR CHESS ACADEMY
- Students can access assigned studies, attendance, class schedules and fee history.
- Coaches can start and conduct live classes, broadcast studies and follow student activity.
- Join live classes with an integrated chessboard and classroom meeting experience.

MAKE IT YOUR BOARD
- Choose from multiple board and piece themes.
- Track ratings, achievements, subscriptions and academy progress.
- Enjoy an Indian-civilisation-inspired interface designed especially for mobile.

Some features require a ChessPerfect academy membership, an internet connection, or an eligible plan. Digital purchases made in the Android app are processed by Google Play.
